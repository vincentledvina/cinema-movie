MOVIE brings aurora chasers, amateur radio operators, and members of the public into the CINEMA mission as contributors to its science. Volunteers will photograph the aurora during planned campaigns, run ground instruments that record continuously, and label auroral forms in the satellites' movies. MOVIE will announce campaigns with enough notice to plan for, give clear instructions, publish each volunteer's data with their name on it, and report the results back to the volunteers who contributed them.
