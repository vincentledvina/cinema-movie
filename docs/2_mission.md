MOVIE brings aurora chasers, amateur radio operators, and members of the public into the CINEMA mission as contributors to its science. Volunteers will photograph the aurora during planned campaigns, run ground instruments that record continuously, and label auroral forms in the satellites' movies. MOVIE's job is to make each of those contributions count: campaigns announced with enough notice to plan for, clear instructions, data published with the volunteer's name on it, and results reported back to the people who made them possible.
