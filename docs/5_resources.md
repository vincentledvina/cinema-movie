Observing guides, graphics, and visualization tools for MOVIE volunteers will be collected here as they are ready, along with MOVIE's results and publications.

## Space weather and aurora data

- [NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC)](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov): space weather forecasts, geomagnetic storm watches, and real-time solar wind data.
- [University of Calgary Auroral Imaging Group](https://www.ucalgary.ca/aurora): all-sky camera images and keograms (a north-south slice of the sky plotted over time) from across Canada.

## Data archives

- [HamSCI community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/hamsci): datasets from HamSCI campaigns, including the Meteor Scatter QSO Party.
- [CINEMA-MOVIE community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/cinema-movie): where MOVIE campaign images will be published.

## Amateur radio

- [HamSCI](https://hamsci.org)
- [Personal Space Weather Station network](https://pswsnetwork.eng.ua.edu/home)

## Results and publications

Campaign summaries, volunteer datasets, publications, and an image gallery will be posted here as MOVIE campaigns produce them. Every volunteer dataset published in the [CINEMA-MOVIE community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/cinema-movie) will be listed with its DOI (digital object identifier).
