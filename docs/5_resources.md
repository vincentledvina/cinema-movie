Observing guides, graphics, and visualization tools for MOVIE volunteers will be collected here as they are ready.

## Space weather and aurora data

- [NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov): forecasts, watches, and real-time solar wind data.
- [University of Calgary Auroral Imaging Group](https://www.ucalgary.ca/aurora): all-sky camera data and keograms from across Canada.

## Data archives

- [HamSCI community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/hamsci): datasets from HamSCI campaigns, including the Meteor Scatter QSO Party.
- [CINEMA-MOVIE community on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/cinema-movie): where MOVIE campaign images will be published.

## Amateur radio

- [HamSCI](https://hamsci.org)
- [Personal Space Weather Station network](https://pswsnetwork.eng.ua.edu/home)
