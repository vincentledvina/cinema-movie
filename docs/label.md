Anyone with a phone or a computer will be able to help CINEMA by labeling the aurora in movies taken by the satellites' auroral imagers. The labeling app shows a short movie of the aurora along with a label, and you swipe to agree or disagree with the label. No account will be needed to start.

Your labels will help check the CINEMA team's automated identifications of auroral forms (the shapes the aurora takes, such as arcs, beads, and streamers) and flag unusual patterns, and the labeled set of movies will be used for all three of MOVIE's [science questions]({{ "/goals/" | relative_url }}).

The labeling app is being designed now. Before the satellites launch, it may be tested on movies from ground-based all-sky cameras. A link to the app, along with tutorials and a guide to the auroral forms you will see, will be posted here when it is ready.
