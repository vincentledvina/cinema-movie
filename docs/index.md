MOVIE (Multi-platform Observations from Volunteers: Ionospheric Experiments) is the citizen science component of CINEMA (Cross-scale INvestigation of Earth's Magnetotail and Aurora), a NASA Small Explorer mission led by Dartmouth College. Nine small satellites will image the aurora from orbit and measure the magnetic field and particles above it, and MOVIE volunteers on the ground will add what the satellites cannot see by themselves.

The CINEMA-MOVIE GitHub site is where MOVIE posts its observing campaigns, the instructions for taking part, and the results that volunteers make possible. There are three ways to take part:

- **Photograph the aurora** during MOVIE campaigns, if you are an aurora chaser. See [Campaigns]({{ "/campaigns/" | relative_url }}) and [Observing Guidance]({{ "/observing/" | relative_url }}).
- **Host an instrument**, if you are a licensed amateur radio operator who can run a Personal Space Weather Station. See [Host an Instrument]({{ "/host/" | relative_url }}).
- **Label aurora** in movies from the satellites, which anyone can do from a phone or a browser. See [Label Aurora]({{ "/label/" | relative_url }}).

CINEMA is scheduled to launch no earlier than 2030, and MOVIE is in its planning phase now. An advisory board of aurora chasers and amateur radio operators is helping plan the campaigns. The email list and discussion forum for volunteers will open later in the mission, and this site will announce the date.
